Objective: To understand how to generate sound output using a buzzer with Arduino.

Components Used: Arduino UNO, Buzzer, Jumper Wires

Output: Buzzer turns ON for 1 second and OFF for 1 second, repeating continuously.

Learning Outcome:

Understood how a buzzer converts an electrical signal into sound.
Reinforced digitalWrite() and delay() for timed outputs.
Learned the difference between active and passive buzzers.
Understood layering audio alerts onto existing digital-output logic.

Challenges Faced:

Confused buzzer polarity, resulting in no sound.
Used steady HIGH instead of tone/frequency for a passive buzzer.
Volume too loud/quiet depending on supply voltage.

Real-World Applications: Audible alarms, appliance alerts, vehicle reversing/parking alert sounds.

Connection to PoC: Used as an audible alert — sounding on entry/exit, or warning a driver approaching an already-occupied slot.
